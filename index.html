This Privacy Policy explains how Payly (the “App”) handles information when you use the App. Payly is a salary calculation utility that converts pay rates, estimates take-home income, compares job offers, and lets you save calculation scenarios.

1. Information handled by the App

Information stored only on your device

Payly stores the following information locally on your device when you enter or save it:

  • salary amounts and selected pay periods;
  • work schedule assumptions, such as hours per day, days per week, and weeks per year;
  • estimated tax rate, bonus, overtime, and unpaid-time settings;
  • saved calculation names and calculation results; and
  • whether you have completed the onboarding screens.

This information is used only to provide the App’s calculation, comparison, and saved-scenario features. It is not sent to the developer or to RevenueCat. You can delete individual saved calculations in the App. You can remove the remaining locally stored information by deleting the App from your device.

Purchase information

Payly offers an optional one-time In-App Purchase called Payly Pro. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your full payment card or bank account details.

Payly uses RevenueCat to load the available product, validate purchases, determine whether Payly Pro is unlocked, restore purchases, prevent purchase fraud, and provide purchase-related analytics. For these purposes, RevenueCat may process:

  • an automatically generated anonymous App User ID;
  • purchase history and transaction or receipt information;
  • the purchased product, purchase status, and entitlement status; and
  • limited technical purchase context, such as platform, app version, locale, and currency.

Payly does not require an account and does not send your name, email address, salary inputs, or saved calculations to RevenueCat. The anonymous App User ID is not intentionally linked by Payly to your real-world identity.

For more information, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

2. How information is used

Information handled through Payly is used to:

  • perform salary calculations and display estimates;
  • save and restore your preferences and calculation scenarios on your device;
  • offer, process, validate, and restore Payly Pro;
  • maintain access to purchased features;
  • detect and prevent purchase fraud;
  • troubleshoot purchase issues; and
  • understand purchase performance through RevenueCat’s purchase analytics.

Payly does not use your information for third-party advertising, behavioral advertising, or tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies.

3. Sharing of information

Payly does not sell or rent personal information.

Purchase-related information may be processed by:

  • Apple, which operates the App Store and processes payments; and
  • RevenueCat, Inc., which provides purchase validation, entitlement management, restoration, and purchase analytics.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies and are expected to provide protections consistent with applicable law. Information may also be disclosed if required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the developer, or the integrity of the App.

4. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored calculation data remains on your device until you delete the relevant saved calculation, clear the App’s data through device controls where available, or uninstall the App.

Purchase records and the anonymous App User ID may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat for as long as necessary to provide purchase restoration and entitlement access, prevent fraud, comply with accounting or legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Their retention practices are governed by their respective privacy policies and legal obligations.

To request access to or deletion of purchase-related information controlled by the developer, contact ronim1studio@gmail.com. The developer may need information that allows the relevant anonymous purchase record to be located. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary to preserve proof of purchase and restoration rights.

You may also manage App Store purchase information through your Apple Account and submit privacy requests to Apple through Apple’s privacy services.

5. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise a right relating to information controlled by the developer, contact ronim1studio@gmail.com. Because Payly does not require an account and uses anonymous purchase identifiers, the developer may have limited ability to identify information as belonging to a particular person without additional purchase-support details.

6. Security

Payly is designed to minimize data collection. Salary and calculation information stays on your device. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used for information processed in connection with purchases. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

Payly is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a purchase-support request, contact ronim1studio@gmail.com so the matter can be reviewed.

8. International processing

Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase-related information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, they use legal safeguards for international data transfers as described in their privacy documentation.
